# Aurora — credits

Songo in the style of **Aurora**, a theme for [muOS](https://muos.dev) by
Rosabel (code by cybardev and harryarry), from the muOS theme catalogue. Its
look follows Aurora: the colours, the navy pills and panels, the sliding
row of gradient cards on the main menu with Settings and Exit below, the
counter pill and the hint pills.

No Aurora files are included: its wallpapers were only looked at for their
colours and layout. The cards' gradients are drawn in code from colours
picked from them, and the icons in `assets/icons/` were drawn for this
theme in Aurora's outlined, two-colour style (the player controls are the
Spruce theme's).
